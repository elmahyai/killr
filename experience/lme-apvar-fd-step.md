`lme` apVar with a variance function is finite-difference noise, not a formula check. `lmeApVar` takes `fdHess` of the full log-likelihood over the reStruct natural parameters, the varStruct parameters, and `lSigma`. The step is `relStep = eps^(1/3)`. Changing the step alone moves the Python result on `varPower(~age)` for Orthodont: the default 6e-6 gives diag 0.4198, 3e-6 gives 0.4454 (12% off R), and 1e-5 gives 0.4240. A 1e-8 match needs bit-exact criterion values, not a formula change.

Two pitfalls hit on the way. (1) The reStruct-only `pdNatural` path raised "pdMatrix for class 'pdNatural' is not translated", and the caller swallowed it into `"not available"`, so default apVar silently returned no matrix. Build the factor from `pdFactor(solve(reStruct))` without `pdMatrix`. (2) The lme result already appends the apVar matrix in `numeric_lme`. Adding it again in a proof doubles the length.
