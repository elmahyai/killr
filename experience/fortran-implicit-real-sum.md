A Fortran routine with no declaration for `sum` or `sum1` gets REAL*4 from implicit typing. pan.f's `gls` has neither, so its beta and sigma2 partial sums round to single precision. A double-precision port then differs from R by about 1e-7 in beta, and 1e-14 is only reached after each partial sum is rounded to float32 the same way. Read the declaration list of every routine before porting a sum.
