CRAN 1.0-9

All six exports of truncnorm 1.0-9 are checked. The density and cdf use extraDistr's normal functions, which are math.erf. The mean uses SciPy's log cdf so a far tail does not underflow. Draws use extraDistr's generators on boot's Mersenne-Twister. There is no crate.
