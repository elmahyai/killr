All exports of polynom 1.4-1 are checked, including derivative, prediction, roots, summary, and the plot curve. Coefficient arithmetic stays in Python. Roots are NumPy eigenvalues of the companion matrix. Orthogonal polynomials use NumPy's QR, matching stats::poly. plot draws the curve with Matplotlib. There is no crate.
