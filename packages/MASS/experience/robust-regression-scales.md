`lqs` adjusts only when `adjust` and `intercept` are both true; the formula method drops the intercept column and sets `intercept` from that. `stdres` hats come from the weighted QR. `rlm` `mad(resid, 0)` centers at 0. `qda` scaling is LINPACK `qr` and `backsolve`. `psi.huber` at 0 is 1 because `k/abs(0)` is `Inf`. `cov.trob` uses the `X` from the start of the converging iteration.
