`shapeo` and `uncholf` are Fortran routines in mclust `mclust.f`, but the `mclust_kernels` binding in `crates/mclust` does not export them. `cvmclustda` exports `mseee`, `eseee` and `uncholf` only. The density kernels `es1e`, `eseii`, `eseei`, `eseev` and `esevi` are in `mclust_kernels`, so the cdens modules call them there and `cdensEEE` calls `eseee` from `cvmclustda`. The full `mclust.f` build with both routines is `crates/mclustbootstraplrt/libmclust_fortran.so`, which `mclust/_fortran.py` loads. Call them through `mclust._fortran.lib()`, as `mclust/_me_common.py` does.
