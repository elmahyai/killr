In plot.rqss the uniform band divides the p x 399 matrix BX by `sqrt(apply(BX^2, 2, sum))`, a vector of 399 column norms. R does not divide each column by its own norm: it recycles the vector down the whole matrix, column-major, so entry k is divided by norm[k mod 399]. A per-column division gave kappa 4.989 where R gives 5.148, and the uniform bands were off by about 0.002. Keep the recycling in the port (ravel column-major, index modulo the vector length), and check any `M / v` in R where `v` is a vector of the wrong length for `M`.
