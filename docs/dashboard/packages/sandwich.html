All 33 exports and the four datasets of sandwich 3.1-3 are translated. Covariance matrices are NumPy. zoo is only the index and coredata used by estfun and the HAC estimators, and combn for multi-way clusters is local. stats supplies lm, glm, and the OLS autoregression. Methods for survival, pscl, mlogit, nnet, clm, gam, rlm, and polr objects are not included.
