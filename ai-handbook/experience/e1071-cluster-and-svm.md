`lca` class probabilities are uniforms filled down columns, and ties use `boot.rng`, not `argmax`. `tune` fold indexes are positions in `perm.ind`, not a set difference. `matchClasses` negative subscripts drop positions. `naiveBayes` character predictors have `nlevels` 0. `ica` initial `W` is filled down columns. `interpolate` convex weights are vectorized across dimensions. `stft` values follow column order. `dist` inside e1071 is `proxy::dist`, which is not in the registry, so those exports stay open. `fclustIndex` separation uses base `dist`. `svm` predict rescales `newdata` and passes coefficients in Fortran order. `tune` ranges vary the first parameter fastest, as `expand.grid` does, and a label error is one minus `classAgreement` on `table(pred, true)`, which sums the diagonal over the levels that both dimnames share, matched by label and not by position. `tune` folds come from `cut(1:n, breaks = cross)`, which is right-closed with both ends widened by a thousandth of the range, so an integer on an interior break goes to the lower fold. The translated `tuning.py` does all three; `tune_grid.py` and `tune_error.py` are no longer needed for these. `centers.bclust` gives a row of NaN for a group with no members, as R's `apply(..., mean)` does; the private `_centers_bclust` in `bclust.py` fills zeros instead, and `e1071/bclust_steps.py` follows R.

`hclust.bclust` stores `cmdscale(dist(allcenters))` when `docmdscale=TRUE`; reconstruct the lower-triangle proximity and use double-centred squared distances. NumPy's eigensolver may choose the opposite eigenvector sign from R, so orient each returned coordinate by its last non-zero entry.
