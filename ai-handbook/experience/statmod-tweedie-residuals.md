`tweedie` deviance uses raw `y` for `kappa` unless `p == 2`. `qres.tweedie` uses `pinvgauss` at `p = 3`, SciPy at 0, 1, and 2, and a series for `1 < p < 2`. Powers above 2 other than the inverse Gaussian are not implemented. `qres.gamma` is log `pgamma` then `qnorm` and does not draw uniforms. `qres.pois` and `qres.binom` share the power-test Mersenne Twister. `qinvgauss` Newton runs at unit mean. `rinvgauss` draws the infinite-mean normals before the main `rnorm` block. `gauss.quad(0, ...)` returns empty before `match.arg`. Weights are `exp(lnmuzero + 2*log(abs(z)))`. `Digamma(link=2)` is probit, not a square. `varfun.digamma` must not call the `d2cumulant` patch. `glm.scoretest` residuals use `qr` of `qr.X`. `glmnb.fit` damped steps use `dpstrf` and `triu`. `remlscoregamma` standard errors invert the information directly. `mixedModel2` needs a complete QR. `compareTwoGrowthCurves` permutes with `boot.rng.sample_int` and counts ties as `+0.5`. `expectedDeviance` Chebyshev tables come from the tarball C file.
`qresid` and `qresiduals` are one R body. Dispatch reads `family$family`, maps a `Negative Binomial` prefix to `nbinom`, and falls through to `qres.default` for `gaussian`.
