R's `x^y` on x86-64 Linux does not call the double `pow`. `R_pow` calls `powl` (long double) and rounds to double, so it can differ from `math.pow` or `np.power` on doubles by one ulp. For `lme(weights = varPower(~age))` that one-ulp weight gap changes the nlminb finite-difference path (objective values differ at about 1e-14, and the fit moves about 6e-9 in sigma). Reproduce R with `numpy.longdouble` power, cast back to float64. This is what `_r_pow` in `packages/nlme/nlme/lme_family.py` does. `exp` and `log` stay on the double libm functions in R, so they need no change.
