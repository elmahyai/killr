A straight line or a constant has `spectrum0.ar` size 0, including a line with a missing row. `batchSE` divides by the original `niter`, not the truncated batch count. `nchain` is 1 or `len`. `rejectionRate` is the mean of unchanged neighbors. `gelman.diag` covariance treats rows as variables. `acf` divides every lag by `n` and scales by the lag-zero covariance. `read.coda` drops the index header and uses inclusive 1-based ranges. `read.jags` derives the `.ind` path and calls `read.coda`. `coda.options` of one character vector returns only the first name. `heidel.diag` estimates the spectral density on the second half, then windows until `pcramer` accepts.
