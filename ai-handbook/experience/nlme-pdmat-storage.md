`matrix()` of a `pdMat` returns the stored parameters, not the matrix. `pdLogChol` stores the log of the upper Cholesky diagonal, then the upper off-diagonal. `pdSymm` stores the upper triangle of half the matrix logarithm. `pdDiag` and `pdIdent` store `log(sd)`. `pdCompSymm` stores `log((1 + rho) / (1 - rho))`, not `atanh(rho)`. `logDet` depends only on the matrix, so any square root gives the same singular values. `pdmat.py` uses the lower Cholesky and the full logarithm, so its `pdLogChol` off-diagonal is 0 and its `pdSymm` is twice R's. `matrix_assign.py` follows R.
