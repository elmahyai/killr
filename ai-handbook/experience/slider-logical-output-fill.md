A slide allocates its result with `slider_init()`, which fills a logical vector with `NA_LOGICAL`. Positions that a `step` skips, and index positions left outside a `complete = FALSE` trim, keep that fill, so they are `NA`, not `FALSE`. `slide_all(c(TRUE, FALSE, NA, TRUE, NA, FALSE), before = 0, step = 2)` is `TRUE NA NA NA NA NA`, and `slide_index_all(c(TRUE, FALSE, TRUE), 1:3, before = 3, complete = TRUE)` is `NA NA NA`. Numeric results use `NA_REAL` the same way.
