`glsApVar` needs the compiled `gls_loglik` QR. A Python LINPACK port can match the likelihood and still miss `apVar`. The kernel is `gls_loglik` and `gls_estimate` in nlme `src/nlmefit.c`, on the `dqrdc2` QR in `src/matrix.c`, so it belongs in a crate and not in Python. The Python port in `glsapvar.py` misses R by about 8e-5 on the Oats AR(1) case. `glsEstimate` and `glsApVar` both call `recalc` on the model structure, so they wait until `recalc` is translated and do not copy a private version of it.

`QR()` seeds `pivot` with `0 .. ncol-1` and calls `dqrdc2` at `sqrt(.Machine$double.eps)`. `gls_loglik` subtracts from the incoming log-likelihood; a short rank stores `-DBL_MAX` and leaves `lRSS` unchanged. `gls_estimate` packs `varBeta` with leading dimension `rank - 1`.

The 8e-5 gap is not a LINPACK logic error. R's `dqrdc2` calls the system BLAS (OpenBLAS 0.3.29 here) for `dnrm2` and `ddot`. OpenBLAS `dnrm2` matched a long-double sum of squares then square root in 300 of 300 trials, while the reference scaled `dnrm2` matched in 124 of 300. Its `ddot` uses SIMD ordering that `numpy.dot` reproduces and a sequential loop does not. A scratch Python `dqrdc2` with those two choices reproduced R's `lRSS` at six sample points and R's Oats `glsApVar` to 2.8e-17. A finite-difference Hessian amplifies one-ulp `lRSS` differences by about 1e-4, which is why a 1e-16 kernel gap shows up at 1e-4 in `apVar`. The fix belongs in the `nlmegls` crate (`dnrm2` and `ddot` must match R's BLAS), not in Python. The crate now calls the system `libblas` `dnrm2_`, `ddot_`, and `daxpy_` from `dqrdc2`, and `dnrm2`/`ddot` alone left 25 of 400 random QR cases one ulp off R. Calling BLAS `daxpy` for the update loop in `dqrdc2` removed the rest, so R's `daxpy` rounds differently from a plain Rust loop (likely FMA). With all three, 400 of 400 `l_rss` match R bit for bit, and `glsApVar` matches with a max abs diff of 0.

The same ulp sensitivity applies to `corSymm` with `natural = TRUE`, which `glsApVar` and `intervals` convert to `corNatural`. Three details must agree bit for bit, or the apVar is off by about 1e-4. (1) R's `symm_fullCorr` sums with a sequential `d_dot_prod`, so `corfactor.symm_full_corr` must not use `np.dot`, which can move the last correlation by one ulp. (2) LINPACK `dpofa` and `dtrsl` call BLAS `ddot`, so `nlmerecalc` calls `libblas` `ddot_` in those two routines, as `nlmegls` does for `dqrdc2`. A plain loop changes 4 of 36 finite-difference design points. (3) A fixture written by `jsonlite` with `digits = NA` keeps only 15 digits and moves the parameters by about 5e-15. Write fixture numbers with `sprintf("%.17g")`. With all three, the `corSymm` apVar matches R with a max abs diff of 0.
